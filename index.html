FAQ

Fair questions

Is wrnty free?
Yes — you can track up to two warranties for free, with receipts, product details and organisation all included. Premium is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that removes the item limit and adds expiry alerts, iCloud sync and PDF export.
Is it really a one-time purchase?
Yes. You pay once and it's yours — no monthly fee, no annual renewal, and no free trial that quietly turns into a charge.
Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up and nothing to log into. Your warranties stay on your device and sync only through your own iCloud if you choose to turn it on.
Will it remind me before a warranty runs out?
That's the whole idea. Set the expiry date and wrnty alerts you in good time, on your Home and Lock Screen, so you can claim while the cover still stands. Expiry alerts are part of Premium.
Can I use it for insurance and warranty claims?
Yes. Keep the receipt, serial number and photos on each item, then export a tidy PDF pack when you need to make a claim to a retailer or insurer.
Which devices does it need?
wrnty runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and syncs between your devices through iCloud. Your data stays private — see the privacy policy.
